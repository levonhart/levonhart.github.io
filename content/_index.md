## Short bio

I am a PhD student at
[LIRMM](https://www.lirmm.fr/),
working on algorithms, complexity, and robust optimization.

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from the [Federal University of Ceará (UFC)](https://mdcc.ufc.br/en/),
with a focus on Algorithms, Graphs, and Optimization,
and a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Mathematics,
with a background in Operations Research and Computational Mathematics.

I am mainly interested in theoretical computer science and combinatorial optimization,
Currently, I am working on game theory in a robust optimization setting,
with focus on sequential decision-making under uncertainty.

## Interests

- Integer and Robust optimization
- Bilevel optimization
- Polyhedral combinatorics and combinatorial optimization
- Algorithms
- Graph Theory
